# Experience corpus and provenance

The bundled 500-story seed corpus is original fictional product data. It contains 375 simulated scenarios and 125 simulated composites: 400 India journeys and 100 international comparators across 15 countries. No record represents a real person, no quotation is presented as testimony, and no source URL is fabricated.

This boundary is deliberate. Publicly accessible stories are not automatically licensed for bulk copying. The product links to authoritative pools so a reader can open publisher-documented accounts without Zysham republishing their text or photographs:

- India Ministry of Education Digital Repository: https://repository.education.gov.in/
- NSDC Skill Impact Bond stories: https://www.nsdcindia.org/sib
- IGNOU Alumni Network: https://alumni.ignou.ac.in/
- UNICEF India YuWaah: https://www.unicef.org/india/economic-opportunities-young-people
- UNICEF South Asia Youth Skills: https://www.unicef.org/rosa/youth-skills-south-asia-stories
- Australia myfuture Career Stories: https://myfuture.edu.au/case-studies
- New Zealand Careers NZ: https://www.careers.govt.nz/maia/
- ILO Skills for Youth Employment: https://www.ilo.org/skills-youth-employment
- WorldSkills impact stories: https://worldskills.org/what/campaigns/impact-skillschangeworlds/impact-stories-worldskills-zambia
- IBM SkillsBuild spotlights: https://skillsbuild.org/spotlights

Before adding a source-backed story, store a short independently written paraphrase plus publisher, direct URL, original title, publication/access dates, country, source type, rights status, transformation note, factual claims with sources, reviewer status, and review-due date. Label it “Source-backed public account (paraphrased)”. Never infer a board, disability, income, identity, or outcome the source does not state.

User submissions need pseudonyms and broad location by default, granular publication consent, moderation, correction/withdrawal/deletion flows, and a guardian process where legally required. Do not collect phone numbers, exact school/class/address, exam identifiers, health records, or another person’s private data. The current prototype stores submitted stories only in the local browser and does not publish them.

Institutional stories can be selective, sponsor-biased, and unable to prove programme causality. Cross-country credentials and outcomes do not transfer cleanly. Always show the source date/country and remind readers that what worked for one learner may not transfer.
