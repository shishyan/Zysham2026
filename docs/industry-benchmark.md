# Career-guidance industry benchmark

Updated: 08 August 2026

“Top five” here means a benchmark set of established, feature-rich platforms—not a market-share ranking. No authoritative global ranking exists across school systems and regions.

## 1. Xello

Best pattern: a guided journey connecting self-discovery, career profiles, goals, course planning, experience tracking, and family participation.

ZYSHAM implementation: the continuous **Compass → Explore → Compare → Roadmap → Evidence** loop and a family perspective that supports rather than overrides the student.

Sources: [Xello feature gallery](https://xello.world/en/features-gallery/), [Xello for families](https://help.xello.world/en-gb/Content/Get-Started/Xello-Families/GS_Families.htm)

## 2. YouScience Brightpath

Best pattern: bringing multiple kinds of personal evidence into career matching, then explaining the educational and work-based pathways around the result.

ZYSHAM implementation: a transparent, self-reflective Career Compass covering interests, observed strengths, subjects, values, and work style. It deliberately makes no psychometric or aptitude claim.

Sources: [YouScience Brightpath](https://www.youscience.com/education/brightpath/), [Why YouScience](https://www.youscience.com/why-choose-youscience/)

## 3. Unifrog

Best pattern: impartial option comparison plus a record of activities, skills, and application evidence.

ZYSHAM implementation: a three-way career comparison surface and Evidence Wallet for projects, conversations, achievements, experiments, and reflections.

Sources: [Unifrog for schools](https://www.unifrog.org/uk-schools), [Unifrog guide for parents](https://www.unifrog.org/know-how/what-is-unifrog-a-guide-for-parents-and-caregivers)

## 4. PowerSchool Naviance CCLR

Best pattern: keeping career discovery connected to course choices, milestones, applications, and the school workflow.

ZYSHAM implementation: a living Grade 10–12 roadmap connecting stream reflection, syllabus rhythm, career experiments, boards, entrances, and applications.

Sources: [Naviance overview](https://go.powerschool.com/rs/861-RMI-846/images/Naviance_Overview_Flyer.pdf), [PowerSchool CCLR/Naviance update](https://www1.powerschool.com/news/powerschool-cclr-naviance-is-recognized-with-tech-learnings-best-of-show-award-at-istelive-2026/)

## 5. SchooLinks

Best pattern: continuously surfacing a useful next action and turning real-world experiences, credentials, and family participation into visible progress.

ZYSHAM implementation: the “Next best step” card, low-risk career experiments, weekly tasks, Evidence Wallet, and Family Room.

Sources: [SchooLinks platform](https://www.schoolinks.com/platform), [SchooLinks work-based learning](https://www.schoolinks.com/platform/student-experience-wbl)

## Originality boundary

ZYSHAM recreates broad product patterns from scratch. It does not copy assessment questions, scoring models, proprietary datasets, career copy, visual layouts, admission predictions, opportunity databases, or branded frameworks from these products.

Its distinct combination is:

> Know yourself → compare paths → try one → reflect → update the plan → discuss with family

This is combined with ZYSHAM’s existing honest stream reality reflection, CBSE context, Miso/Ollie mentor voices, syllabus and entrance planning, and public Prompt Journal.
